# Social Engineering & Psychological Manipulation

## Overview
Social engineering is the psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information. Rather than exploiting technical software bugs, it exploits human cognitive biases, instincts, and emotional reflexes.

## Core Psychological Triggers (Cialdini's Principles of Influence)
1. **Urgency & Artificial Time Pressure**:
   - Forcing rapid action ("within 15 minutes", "offer expires today") to bypass rational analytical thinking and trigger instinctive panic reactions.
2. **Fear & Coercion**:
   - Threats of legal action, incarceration, account freezing, or public humiliation ("Digital arrest", "FIR registered", "CBI raid").
3. **Authority & Pretexting**:
   - Adopting symbols of institutional authority (police uniforms, judicial warrants, official logos, formal legal jargon).
4. **Greed & Scarcity**:
   - Promises of exclusive lottery jackpots, guaranteed cryptocurrency yields, or high-paying effortless remote jobs.
5. **Sympathy & Reciprocity**:
   - Giving small early rewards (e.g., small task scam payouts of Rs 300) to obligate the victim to reciprocate with larger deposits.

## Common Attack Contexts
- **Smishing (SMS Phishing)**: Urgent KYC, unpaid electricity bill, or bank suspension texts.
- **Vishing (Voice Phishing)**: Impersonating bank fraud desks, police officers, or customs officials.
- **Spear Phishing**: Targeted attacks customized using data scraped from LinkedIn, Instagram, or corporate websites.

## Counter-Techniques (Psychological Firewalls)
- **The 10-Minute Cooling Rule**: Whenever a communication triggers intense urgency, fear, or excitement, deliberately pause for 10 minutes before taking any action.
- **Independent Channel Validation**: Never rely on contact details supplied within the suspicious communication. Independently look up the institution's official helpline.
