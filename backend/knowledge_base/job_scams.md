# Fake Employment & Part-Time Task Scams

## Overview
Part-time job and recruitment scams target students, freelancers, and job seekers by offering high hourly wages for trivial online tasks (e.g., liking YouTube videos, rating Google Maps listings, hotel review submissions).

## The Task Scam Pipeline
1. **Initial Outreach**:
   - Unsolicited WhatsApp/Telegram message from a self-proclaimed HR manager from a well-known brand (Amazon, YouTube, Marriott, Telegram).
   - "Earn Rs 3,000 to Rs 8,000 daily from home by spending 30 minutes liking videos."
2. **The Hook (Small Real Payouts)**:
   - The victim is assigned 3 easy tasks and receives a genuine small payment of Rs 200–500 directly via UPI to build trust.
3. **The Trap (Prepaid Task Investment)**:
   - The victim is invited to join a "VIP Telegram Task Group".
   - Higher-paying tasks now require advance "prepaid security deposits" (e.g., pay Rs 5,000 to earn Rs 7,500; pay Rs 50,000 to earn Rs 80,000).
4. **The Frozen Withdrawal Lock**:
   - Once a large deposit is made, the scammer claims the user made an "algorithmic data input error" and must deposit an additional Rs 100,000 to unlock their principal.

## Red Flags
- Recruitment conducted entirely through WhatsApp, Telegram, or unauthorized email domains (Gmail/Hotmail instead of corporate enterprise domains).
- Demands for registration fees, background check fees, equipment deposits, or task balance funding.
- Daily earnings that are disproportionate to the simplicity of the task.

## Action Steps
- Legitimate employers never ask candidates to pay money or transfer funds to secure employment or complete work tasks.
- Cease all communication immediately upon any request for advance payments.
