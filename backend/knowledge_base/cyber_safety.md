# Cyber Safety, Incident Response & Reporting Protocols

## Overview
Effective cyber defense combines preventative digital hygiene with immediate, disciplined incident containment protocols when an attack or fraudulent transaction occurs.

## Immediate Post-Incident Containment (The Golden Hour)
1. **Financial Fraud Incident (First 1–2 Hours)**:
   - Immediately call the **National Cyber Crime Reporting Helpline at 1930** (India) or your nation's emergency financial fraud line.
   - The 1930 system links directly to the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System (CFCFRMS), enabling law enforcement to freeze funds in transit across the banking and UPI settlement pipeline before fraudsters withdraw cash at ATMs.
   - Call your bank's 24x7 emergency helpline to freeze compromised accounts, block net-banking access, and cancel debit/credit cards.
2. **Device Compromise (Malicious APK or Screen Sharing Installed)**:
   - Turn on **Airplane Mode** immediately and disconnect from Wi-Fi.
   - Remove the physical SIM card to prevent incoming SMS interception.
   - Back up crucial personal files, then perform a complete Factory Reset to eradicate resident persistent malware.
3. **Account Credential Exposure**:
   - Change passwords immediately from a separate, clean device.
   - Terminate all active sessions via account security settings ("Sign out of all other sessions").
   - Enable hardware or authenticator app-based Two-Factor Authentication (avoiding SMS-based 2FA where possible).

## Formal Reporting Procedures
- **Official Online Portal**: Lodge an official cybercrime complaint at `https://cybercrime.gov.in`.
- **Evidence Preservation**:
  - Keep full, uncropped screenshots of SMS messages, WhatsApp chats, transaction receipts, call logs, and browser history.
  - Record the fraudster's phone numbers, UPI VPAs, bank account numbers, IFSC codes, and transaction reference numbers (UTR / RRN).
  - Do not delete suspicious messages, as digital forensic investigators rely on raw message headers and metadata.

## Standard Digital Hygiene Checklist
- Never install applications from outside official app stores (`.apk` direct download links).
- Never share OTPs, CVVs, PINs, or net-banking passwords with anyone.
- Disable auto-download of media in messaging apps.
- Regularly review connected devices in Google, Apple, and WhatsApp accounts.
