# Unified Payments Interface (UPI) Scams

## Overview
UPI fraud exploits consumer misunderstandings of payment flows on platforms such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM to deceive users into transferring funds to attacker-controlled accounts.

## Common Scam Patterns
1. **The "Scan to Receive Money" QR Code Trap**:
   - The fraudster tells a seller on OLX or Facebook Marketplace that they want to send an advance payment.
   - The scammer sends a QR code claiming, "Scan this QR code to receive Rs 15,000 in your account."
   - **Critical Rule**: Scanning a QR code and entering your UPI PIN always DEBITS money from your account. You NEVER need to enter a PIN to receive money.
2. **UPI Collect Request Deception**:
   - Scammers initiate a "Collect Request" for a specific amount.
   - They disguise the request with descriptions such as "Refund Processed" or "Cashback Approved" and trick the user into approving and typing their UPI PIN.
3. **Overpayment & Refund Illusion**:
   - The scammer sends a fake SMS screenshot showing an accidental transfer of excess money and begs for a refund before the victim checks their actual bank balance.
4. **Spoofed Payment Confirmation Apps**:
   - Attackers use fake payment soundbox APKs or fake payment receipt generator apps showing successful payment checkmarks.

## Key Red Flags
- Any instruction requesting you to enter your **UPI PIN** to receive a cashback, reward, lottery, or buyer payment.
- Unsolicited payment collect notifications from unknown Virtual Payment Addresses (VPAs).
- Requests to make a "test payment of Rs 1" to verify your UPI account.

## Safe Handling Protocols
- **Golden Rule of UPI**: Entering a UPI PIN = Sending Money. Receiving money requires zero authorization from the receiver.
- Verify payment arrival strictly inside your official banking/UPI app transaction history, never by relying on SMS or screenshots sent by a counterparty.
- Immediately report fraudulent VPAs inside the UPI application and register an alert with your bank's fraud monitoring team.
