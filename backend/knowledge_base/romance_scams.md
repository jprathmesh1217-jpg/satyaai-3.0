# Romance & Emotional Extortion Scams

## Overview
Romance scams exploit emotional vulnerability and romantic longing to trick individuals into wiring money, purchasing gifts, or paying non-existent customs clearance duties.

## Mechanism & Stages
1. **The Persona**:
   - The scammer creates an attractive profile on dating sites (Tinder, Bumble, Matrimony portals) or social media.
   - Personas often pose as wealthy expatriates, military doctors stationed overseas, maritime engineers, or foreign business executives.
2. **Love Bombing**:
   - The fraudster establishes intense emotional attachment rapidly, professing deep affection and proposing marriage within weeks without meeting in person.
3. **The Crisis / Gift Pretext**:
   - *Pretext A (The Expensive Gift)*: Scammer claims to have sent luxury watches, gold jewellery, or laptops, but an accomplice posing as an Indian Customs / Airport Clearance officer calls demanding "clearance penalties" or "anti-money laundering certification fees".
   - *Pretext B (The Medical Emergency)*: Scammer suddenly claims a catastrophic accident, stolen wallet, or hospital detention in a foreign country and begs for urgent financial assistance.

## Red Flags
- The person repeatedly makes excuses to avoid video calls or in-person meetings (faulty camera, poor signal, military protocol).
- Requests to transfer money to personal bank accounts belonging to third parties.
- Pressure to keep the relationship and financial transactions secret from friends and family.

## Safe Action Guidelines
- Never transfer funds or send gifts to an online acquaintance whom you have never met in real life.
- Perform reverse image searches on profile pictures using Google Lens or TinEye to identify stolen model photos.
