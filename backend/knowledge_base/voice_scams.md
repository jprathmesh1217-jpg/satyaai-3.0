# AI Voice Cloning & Audio Vishing Scams

## Overview
AI voice cloning scams use deep learning acoustic models trained on short audio samples (as little as 3–5 seconds from Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube) to recreate the voice, timbre, cadence, and accent of a specific person for fraudulent voice calls (vishing).

## Common Scenarios
1. **The Family Kidnapping / Accident Emergency**:
   - A parent receives a panic-stricken phone call: "Mom, I've been in a severe car accident and police have detained me. Please wire Rs 50,000 to this clinic right now."
   - The voice matches their child's acoustic signature identically.
2. **Executive Authorization Vishing**:
   - Employees receive a phone call from their CEO or managing director asking for an urgent vendor payment or gift card purchase code.
3. **Automated Robocall Spoofing**:
   - Interactive voice response (IVR) systems mimicking telecom authorities, utility boards, or courier providers requesting confirmation of personal information via keypad tones.

## Forensic Audio Indicators
- **Robotic Flatness & Pitch Quantization**: Subtle metallic undertones, unnatural monotonic pitch stability, or absence of genuine emotional breathing variations.
- **Acoustic Background Disconnect**: Complete silence in the background or unnatural cuts in room reverberation when the speaker starts or pauses talking.
- **Short, Fragmented Speech**: Scammers often keep voice clones talking for only brief sentences before an "accomplice" (e.g., doctor, police officer) grabs the phone to prevent prolonged scrutiny.

## Countermeasures & Family Safety Protocols
- **Establish a Family Safe Word**: Agree with immediate family members on an unguessable private verbal password to verify genuine distress in emergency calls.
- **Call Back Directly**: Disconnect the incoming call and immediately dial the person's standard known mobile number.
- **Ask Challenge Questions**: Ask a specific question about a personal childhood memory or private fact that no online scraper or AI model could know.
