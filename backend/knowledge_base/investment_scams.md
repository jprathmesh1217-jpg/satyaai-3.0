# Investment & Cryptocurrency Fraud

## Overview
Investment scams entice victims with promises of extraordinary returns, risk-free compounding, insider stock tips, or algorithmic cryptocurrency trading bots, ultimately trapping deposits in fake dashboards.

## Common Typologies
1. **WhatsApp / Telegram Trading Groups**:
   - Victims are added to groups claiming to represent institutional funds or celebrity market analysts.
   - Fake group members (bots or shills) flood the chat with screenshots showing massive daily profits.
2. **Fabricated Trading Web Applications**:
   - Victims deposit funds into an online platform. The platform shows synthetic portfolio growth of 200%–500%.
   - When the victim attempts to withdraw funds, the platform blocks withdrawal, demanding 20%–40% in "tax clearance fees", "capital gains deductions", or "security verification collateral".
3. **Pig Butchering (Sha Zhu Pan)**:
   - Starts with a seemingly accidental wrong-number WhatsApp message or dating app match.
   - The scammer builds romantic or friendly rapport over weeks before introducing a "lucrative private crypto exchange".
4. **Pump-and-Dump Stock Schemes**:
   - Unregistered entities push illiquid micro-cap stocks via bulk SMS blasts claiming impending multi-bagger buyouts.

## Key Indicators
- Guarantees of zero financial risk with fixed returns exceeding 2%–10% daily or weekly.
- Instructions to deposit investment capital into personal UPI accounts or rotating individual mule accounts rather than registered SEBI/FINRA institutional brokerage accounts.
- Demands for additional cash deposits in order to release previously invested funds.

## Protection Advice
- Verify the registration of financial advisers and brokers on official regulatory registries (e.g., SEBI in India, SEC in the US).
- If a platform demands payment of fees to withdraw your own money, it is 100% a scam. Cease all deposits immediately.
